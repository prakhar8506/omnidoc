Delete your account

This page is the public way to ask for deletion, including if you no longer have the app installed.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

In the app

Open Cura, tap your profile photo, then Delete Account. That erases the encrypted copy on the phone. An email account is also deleted from the cloud, including uploaded lab files, when the server can complete the request.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email support@cura.health with the subject “Delete my Cura account” and the email address on the account. Google sign-in stores the health profile on the phone only. Uninstalling the app removes that copy.

What remains

Payment records held by the payment provider follow that provider’s rules. Server backups of an email account may keep data briefly before they rotate out. We do not sell health data.
