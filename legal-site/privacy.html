Privacy Policy

Cura — Quiet care for everyday health

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Overview

Cura helps you organize personal health information, wearable trends, appointments, and lab documents. Cura is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment.

Who can use Cura

Cura is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, write to support@cura.health and we will remove it.

Data we collect

Account details you give us (email address, display name, blood type).

Health information you enter: journal reflections, symptoms you describe, mood and mental-health screening answers, meals and water, medications, allergies, chronic conditions, menstrual cycle and pregnancy logs, glucose and blood pressure readings, and emergency contacts.

Wearable metrics you authorize through Apple Health or Health Connect: heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, steps, sleep, oxygen saturation, and blood pressure.

Documents you upload: photos and PDFs of lab reports and prescriptions, and the text read from them.

The city you type when searching for a doctor.

Crash and performance diagnostics, so we can fix errors.

How we use data

We use what you enter to show it back to you and, when you ask, to write a plain-language explanation. Email sign-in can store a copy in your Supabase account. Continue with Google keeps the health profile on this phone and does not create a Supabase account. Information from Health Connect is used only for the features you open in Cura, including the Health Connect Limited Use requirements. We do not sell health data and we do not use it for advertising.

AI processing

When you use AI chat, symptom guidance, or lab report reading, the relevant content is sent to our own server functions and then to an AI provider we contract with — currently OpenAI, with Google Gemini as a fallback — purely to answer that request.

For lab reports this can include the report image itself, or the text read from it, together with a short summary of your medications and recent metrics so the explanation makes sense. Providers process this on our behalf and are not permitted to use it to train their models.

AI output is informational, can be incomplete or wrong, and is never a diagnosis. Confirm anything that matters with a clinician.

Crash and performance reporting

We use Sentry to receive crash reports and performance traces. These contain technical details such as the error, screen, device model, and operating system version. They are not used to identify you and they do not carry your health records.

Storage & security

An email account’s cloud copy is stored in Supabase with row-level security so only that account can read it. The phone copy is encrypted with the device keystore. It is not included in Android cloud backup or device transfer. An app update keeps it. Uninstalling the app removes it. If you choose Continue with Google, Google shares your name and email address with Cura so we can open your account on this phone. We never receive your Google password.

Retention & deletion

Tap your avatar in the top bar, then Delete Account. That erases the encrypted copy on this phone. If you are signed in with email, it also asks our server to delete that cloud account, its rows, and uploaded files. A Google sign-in has no cloud health account to delete. You can also open the deletion page. It cannot be undone. Server backups of an email account may retain data briefly before they rotate out. Write to support@cura.health with the subject “Grievance” for a privacy complaint.

Your choices

You can turn individual wearable categories on or off in the permission centre, export your data as a PDF at any time, and delete your account entirely. Denying a permission does not stop you from using the rest of the app.
