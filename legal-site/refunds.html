Cancellation and refunds

Cura Premium is access inside the app. There is no physical product to return.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Free trial

The 7-day trial does not charge your card. If you do not subscribe, the trial ends and paid features lock. You do not need a refund for a trial you were not charged for.

Paid year

Cura Premium is ₹599 for one year, paid once through Razorpay. The year starts when the payment succeeds.

Email support@cura.health with the subject “Cura Premium refund” within 7 days of the payment if you want the charge reversed. Include the email on your Cura account. We refund the payment to the original method after we confirm it. Premium access ends when the refund is issued.

After those 7 days, the year is not refunded, because the digital access was provided. You can keep using Premium until the year ends. It does not renew by itself.

Failed or duplicate charges

If you were charged twice, or charged but Premium did not unlock, email us with the payment reference. We will either unlock the year or refund the extra charge.

What we do not refund

We do not refund because a watch did not provide a reading, or because a health feature stayed blank when the required data was not on file. Cura does not invent those readings.
