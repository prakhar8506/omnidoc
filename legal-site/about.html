About us

Cura is a personal health app for adults. It helps you keep wearable readings, meals, visits, and documents in one place.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

What Cura is

Cura Health makes the Cura Android app. You sign in, connect a watch or fitness tracker if you have one, and log the health details you choose to keep. Premium adds coaching tools such as lab explanations, Health AI, meal plans, and a workout plan.

Cura is not a hospital, a clinic, or a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. In an emergency, call local emergency services.

Who it is for

Cura is for people aged 18 and over. The paid plan is a digital subscription inside the app. We do not sell or ship physical goods.
